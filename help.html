User Guide

Form Fill Kaise Karein?

Kripya niche diye gaye steps ko dhyan se padhein taaki form easily fill ho sake.

Step 1

Full Name Enter Karein

Apna pura asli naam likhein jo aapke official documents me ho.

Step 2

10-Digit Phone Number

Apna working 10 digits ka mobile number darj karein taaki hum aapse contact kar sakein.

Step 3

Gmail / Email Address

Apna valid Gmail / Email ID add karein jiske zariye official updates diye ja sakein.

Step 4

Business / Store / Brand Name

Apni dukaan, brand ya business ka exact naam type karein.

Step 5

Full Address Fill Karein

Apna complete pata (Address) pincode ke sath likhein. Yeh entry mandatory hai.

Step 6 (Optional)

Additional Note

Agar aap hume koi extra instruction ya message dena chahte hain toh Note section me likhein.

Step 7

Submit Details Par Click Karein

Sabhi compulsory fields fill karne ke baad Submit button enable ho jayega, uspar click karein.
